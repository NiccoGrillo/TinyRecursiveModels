# PH-C · learned case-conditioned factor messages on Sudoku

Registered 28 November 2026 before learning or inspecting any PH-C outcome. On original full-input puzzles, a soft mixture of messages after two proposed digit assumptions should increase final exact solves over an equally trained serial reasoner spending the same number of factor updates. Unlike training a write into a frozen TRM, the underlying constraint dynamics and branch viability are trainable together. The task-specific prior is substantial: the engine receives Sudoku's four classes of exactly-one factors (cell, row-digit, column-digit, box-digit), and a fixed permutation-equivariant max-competitor message primitive. It does not receive deduction traces or solver-selected pivots. Under the dataset's unique-solution assumption, a wrong digit can receive a viability target of zero; it cannot supply a target completed board under that digit. We never train such a completion.

## Engine and controls

Represent each of 729 cell-digit propositions by a unary clue score plus four incident scalar messages. For each factor compute a cavity score (unary plus incoming messages from the other factors), pool the largest competing proposition score in that factor, and update each outbound message through learned coefficients, smooth saturation and damping. Initial coefficients favor a negative competitor as in max-product, and a learned nonlinear residual can alter this rule. Share learned weights across steps, symmetrically across digits/cells; row/column/box/cell can have separate weights. The model is deliberately cheap and strongly structured. The initial clue clamp is fixed. Both models train from scratch on identical streams, including a common early board loss and early pivot-candidate supervision.

Run four updates; on a blank pick the cell whose learned posterior has maximum top-two mass times ambiguity (second-highest probability), then propose its two highest posterior digits. The shared executor runs three further updates under each of the two hypothetical unary clamps. Learn a branch viability logit from the proposal probability and statistics of board confidence/conflict after this computation. Mix the two factor-message states with softmax viability weights, release hypotheses, and execute four more shared updates under the original clues. The strongest streamlined rival uses four initial and ten consecutive unconditioned updates, the same weights, initialization, optimization and candidate viability readout at the midpoint, spending the same 14 update calls. It has more serial depth (14 vs 11). The conditional arm sees a positive-branch-only completion loss; serial receives matched intermediate completion supervision on the same eligible cases. A two-element learned selection policy and per-cell posterior mixture are secondary, cheaper baselines. Clamp-control: recompute both arms of the trained case model without the unary assumption (original observations and both calls retained). This tests dependence on clamping, without attributing a gain purely to message mixing. Also evaluate the same trained model continuing only its highest-scored branch after releasing the clamp.
